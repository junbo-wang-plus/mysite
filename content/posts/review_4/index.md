# [Passion](https://letterboxd.com/film/passion-2008/)
![](passion.jpg)
{{< byline author="Ryusuke Hamaguchi" year="2008" >}}

There is a call for the photography club's magazine and the theme is *Passion*. It has been a month and I'm still trying to find passion for me to put on film. I was randomly flipping through Letterboxd and saw a comment about how this movie is Hamaguchi's version of *Husbands* by Cassavetes. It has been a long time since I last watched it but my memories with the latter is still fresh. So it was inevitable that I rewatched it.

I had forgotten that long before *All of a Sudden*, Hamaguchi was already using literal blackboard (or white) classroom settings in films and they are always one of the most thrilling scenes. This time the lesson was about what to do when faced with violence. All you can do is acceptance and forgiveness. Even if you are killed. Being killed is the ultimate forgiveness. 

I had also forgotten most of the plot so once again I was crushed and amazed at the characters' choices. I think it's fitting because I can never predict what humans do in real life either. The things people do to exert violence and when faced with it are illogical yet make perfect sense. 

Tomoya is such a dipshit yet I feel for him. I guess he's just a little guy.I didn't know Takeshi had it in him. Thank god it was with Takako. I hope Kaho finds a different job. I am Kenichiro.

I'm still looking for passion.
